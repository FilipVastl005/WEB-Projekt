Hardware Strategy

Three Tailored Deployment Topologies

Engineered for diverse connectivity and power constraints

TOPOLOGY 1 Wi-Fi 6 / Urban

Standalone Wi-Fi 6

Urban Edge & High-Density Centers

Powered by an ESP32-C6 utilizing Target Wake Time (TWT) protocols to achieve micro-amp deep sleep states between 15-millisecond transmission bursts.

• Power: Single-cell 3.2V LiFePO₄ with TP5000 solar charging
• Thermal Isolation: MOSFET duty-cycling isolates air quality heaters (MQ-135) to prevent temperature drift
• Telemetry: TLS encrypted MQTT directly to global cloud
Target: Urban apartments, balconies, smart cities
TOPOLOGY 2 Sub-GHz / Home

Sub-GHz + Home Deck

Suburban & Residential Farms

A two-node architecture: outdoor mast node (ESP32-C3 + SX1262 LoRa with Slotted TDMA) transmitting to an indoor ESP32-S3 "Home Deck" hub.

• Local Portal: Built-in web dashboard at http://homedeck.local
• Home Automation: Native Home Assistant MQTT auto-discovery
• Offline Resilience: MicroSD FAT32 logging with automatic corruption recovery
Target: Homesteads, community schools, perimeter gardens
TOPOLOGY 3 Off-Grid / Mesh

Autonomous Mesh Hub

Remote & Underserved Regions

Multi-hop peer-to-peer LoRa mesh nodes operating on ISM bands (868/915MHz) using bit-packed binary C-struct payloads for minimal airtime.

• Community Solar Kiosk: Captive Wi-Fi AP at http://community.weather (free for smartphones)
• Zero Cellular Dependency: Residents view frost alerts and soil moisture without mobile data
• DTN Sync: Delay-Tolerant Networking batches data via field workers' passing devices
Target: Rural smallholders, off-grid villages, remote valleys
Zero-Trust Architecture

Multi-tier Quality Control (QC) & SQI Engine.

Raw sensor data cannot be blindly trusted. Every incoming packet undergoes three automated validation stages before being written to our core TimescaleDB climate repository.

1

Physical Boundary Checks

Drops impossible environmental readings outside physical survival boundaries before entering any database.

• Pressure < 800 hPa → REJECT
• Temperature > 60°C → REJECT
• Humidity < 0% or > 100% → DROP
2

Temporal Rate-of-Change

Identifies and rejects abrupt artificial spikes caused by sudden direct sunlight exposure, drafts, or hardware glitches.

• ΔT > 3.0°C in < 60s → SPIKE FLAGGED
• Unnatural barometric jumps → ISOLATED
• Transient heater bleed → FILTERED
3

Spatial IDW Validation

Inverse Distance Weighting validates each sensor against its N nearest neighbors within a 25km radius.

• Radius: 25 km neighborhood
• Anomaly Threshold: > 3.0 σ
• Action: Station SQI penalty & de-weight

Station Quality Index (SQI)

Score: 0% – 100%

Every station in the network receives a dynamic SQI score. Clean, timely data and consistent uptime elevate the station's trustworthiness.

≥ 80% SQI: Platinum Node Primary NWP Modeling
60% – 79% SQI: Standard Node Included in Public Map
< 60% SQI: Demoted Node Hidden from Global Feed

FAQ

Frequently asked questions.

Technical, logistical, and architectural answers regarding the Mateo platform.

How do off-grid communities access data without cellular signal?
Topology 3 uses a solar-powered community hub that broadcasts a captive Wi-Fi portal (http://community.weather). Anyone nearby can connect with any standard smartphone to check hyper-local frost predictions, barometric pressure, and soil telemetry completely free without SIM cards or cellular data.
How does Mateo prevent sensor heating and inaccurate readings?
We tackle this both in hardware and software. At the hardware level, high-draw sensor heaters (like MQ-135 air quality elements) are physically isolated on separate PCB traces with MOSFET duty-cycling to prevent thermal bleed into the ambient temperature chamber. In software, our Stage 2 temporal filter detects artificial spikes (>3°C in under 60 seconds) typically caused by direct sunlight exposure.
What happens when internet connection is lost on a suburban Home Deck?
The indoor Home Deck node (ESP32-S3) maintains a local web server (http://homedeck.local) and automatically writes all readings to an onboard microSD card as CSV files. It features automated FAT32 corruption recovery and will automatically sync queued historical records once home internet is restored.
What is the Station Quality Index (SQI) and what causes demotion?
The SQI is a real-time credibility metric (0% to 100%) computed for every station. Consistent uptime and spatially congruent readings increase the score. If a station consistently deviates by more than 3 standard deviations from its neighbors within a 25km radius, its SQI drops. Stations falling below 60% SQI are automatically suppressed from public maps and numerical forecasting models.
Is all software, hardware, and data completely free and open-source?
Yes, absolutely. Mateo is built entirely as an open public good. All hardware schematics (KiCad PCB designs, 3D printable Stevenson screens), ESP32 firmware, web dashboards, and quality-control algorithms are licensed under permissive open-source licenses (GPLv3 & CERN-OHL). Anyone can freely access the live map, download historical data archives, or build and deploy their own independent stations without licensing fees or subscriptions.
